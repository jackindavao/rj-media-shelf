Read three new pages each day and reread the pages that came before. The stories are designed to become familiar over several happy readings.

Module 3 · Music and listening

Dorothy at ang Munting Awit

A little bird forgets its song. The friends listen to a waterfall, rustling leaves, a flowing river, and Tik-Tok's steady ticking until the melody returns.

  • 12 short pages
  • 263 Filipino words
  • 7 illustrations
  • Matched beginner level
  • Four-day sequence

For RJ: A sound-filled story supports careful listening, patience, and related verb forms without raising the established reading level. The adult guide includes an expanded practical pronunciation key.
