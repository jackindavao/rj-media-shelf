Module 1 · Nature and patience

Dorothy at ang Munting Binhi

Dorothy finds a tiny seed. The friends search for the right place, plant it together, and discover that growing something takes light, water, care, and patience.

  • 12 short pages
  • 260 Filipino words
  • 7 illustrations
  • Matched beginner level
  • Four-day sequence

For RJ: Familiar language and characters support a new sequence of planting, waiting, and growing. English translation and teaching notes remain in the adult guide.
